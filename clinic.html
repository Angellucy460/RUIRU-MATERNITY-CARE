About Us!

RUIRU MATERNITY CARE is a leading maternity care clinic, providing a full range of maternity care to women and their families. Our team of experienced healthcare professionals is committed to ensuring a safe and comfortable pregnancy journey for every patient.

Welcome to RUIRU MATERNITY CARE where we are all about providing excellent maternity care.

Our clinic is all about supporting the mums and their families right through the pregnancy. With a team of experienced healthcare professionals we offer comprehensive care, personalized attention, and a warm and welcoming environment.

At RUIRU MATERNITY CARE we know that pregnancy is a very special experience. Our aim is to ensure the health of both mother and baby and to provide the right amount of emotional support and guidance throughout the entire process.

Our Activities!

We're very much involved in all sorts of stuff to promote maternal and child health in the community.

Our activities include;

Community Health Education Programs.

We run workshops and seminars to educate mums and their families about pregnancy, birth and the care of the newborn.

These programmes are designed to equip women with the knowledge and skills to make informed choices about their own health and the health of their babies.

Free Health Camps and Screenings.

We run free health camps and screenings to provide healthcare to the communities that we serve.

These help to identify potential health problems early and enable appropriate intervention for the mother.

Support Groups for Expectant Mothers.

We run support groups for expectant mothers where they can meet, share their experiences and get the emotional support they need.

These groups offer a safe space for women to talk about their issues and get advice.

Collaborations with Local Organizations for Maternal Health Initiatives.

We work in partnership with local organisations and NGOs to deliver our maternal health work in the community.

These are collaborations designed to improve access to quality healthcare and to increase awareness of maternal health.
